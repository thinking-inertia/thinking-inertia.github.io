01 / Overview

No-thinking is a behavior of the emitted response.

We split each answer into visible pre-answer text T and final answer A. The central question is deliberately observable: when a model is told to answer directly, what question-conditioned payload still appears before A?

02 / Measurement

Three levels, one observable object.

The hierarchy moves from format compliance to relevance and then to a direct judgment of inference. No single similarity score is treated as a reasoning detector.

All three levels operate on the emitted text. They support claims about visible reasoning and thinking inertia; they do not attempt to infer latent cognition.

Takeaway. “Answer only” is not one uniform switch. The answer interface and intervention mode jointly shape how much visible work survives.

04 / Human study

The hierarchy was checked by people, too.

A unified blinded study complements the automated checks. Five annotators reviewed the same 600 proportion-matched outputs, scored question relevance on a five-point scale, and applied the mutually exclusive reasoning rubric with an exact supporting span.

The human ratings and labels preserve the same metric hierarchy and the central answer-space and intervention comparisons. This validates the measurements and the conclusions they support without turning a response-level audit into a claim about latent cognition.

05 / Reproduce

Start from a clean response trace.

The public release contains the reusable runner, normalized benchmark inputs, parsers, scoring utilities, and QRel./EIR tools. Paper-specific raw corpora and human-review records remain outside the release.
