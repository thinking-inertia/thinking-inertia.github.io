🌟 Overview

When a language model is told not to think, does its emitted answer actually contain no pre-answer work? We split each response into visible pre-answer text T and final answer A, then evaluate the same response at three increasingly specific levels. ETR asks whether T is empty; QRel. asks whether remaining T is relevant to the question; and EIR asks whether T contains an explicit inferential step. Accuracy is measured on A independently. Together, these response-level observables separate strict format compliance, relevance, and visible inference without making claims about latent computation.

🔬 Three levels, one response

Each level answers a different question about the same pair (Q, T). Pick a label to see what it measures. QRel. is an instruction-aware relevance signal; it is not used by itself as a reasoning detector.

ETR — Empty-Thinking Ratio. Strict answer-only compliance: the share of outputs with an empty visible pre-answer field T.

📊 Key findings

The answer-space staircase

Across the three answer interfaces, visible explicit inference follows a stable ordering:

Bool<MCQ<Openvisible inference increases as the answer space opens

Open-ended answers leave the most room for question-conditioned work to survive before the final answer. This staircase remains visible after separating relevance from explicit reasoning.

Thinking inertia under stricter controls

Within each answer space, stricter no-thinking intervention reduces visible inference but does not erase the answer-space effect:

Observable, not latent

The title refers to retaining visible explicit inference when told not to think, not to hidden cognition. ETR, QRel., and EIR describe the emitted response and make its evidence auditable.

📝 Human validation

A unified blinded study complements the automated checks. Five annotators reviewed the same 600 proportion-matched outputs, scored question relevance on a five-point scale, and applied the mutually exclusive reasoning rubric with an exact supporting span.

600outputs

Human ratings and labels preserve the metric hierarchy and the central answer-space and intervention comparisons. This validates the measurements and the conclusions they support without turning a response-level audit into a claim about latent cognition.
